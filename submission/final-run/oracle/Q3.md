## Rules applied
- operations-sop-rev2.md, **OPS-1 Gas odor response**: gas odor report = operator note saying crew smelled gas, rotten-egg smell/gas odor, or an H2S monitor alarmed. Response = maintenance job on that well (`started_at`) OR inspection with `method = manual`; drone inspections do NOT count. Must start after note `created_at` and within 24 h (notes before 1 Jan 2027) / 12 h (from 1 Jan 2027). Revision history: 12 h applies "for reports from 1 Jan 2027".
- operations-sop-rev2.md "Operator notes" section: note `created_at` is the observation time, UTC.
- Area name from `fleet/areas.json` (identical to metadata `area_id` for all 600 wells).
- Scope: `note_type = operator_note` only, `created_at >= 2026-11-01`.

## Counting method
Scanned all 600 `context.jsonl` for operator notes matching `gas smell|gas odor|rotten|h2s|smelled gas` (templates found: "Gas odor reported by the pumper near WELL-X wellhead", "H2S monitor alarmed at WELL-X", "Strong gas odor around the WELL-X cellar", "Rotten-egg smell at the WELL-X pad", "Smelled gas downwind of WELL-X wellhead"). 29 such notes exist overall; 3 predate 1 Nov and are excluded (WELL-0147 CTX-0147-001 2026-10-18, WELL-0155 CTX-0155-001 2026-10-19, WELL-0077 CTX-0077-001 2026-10-25) leaving 26. For each, took the earliest maintenance `started_at` or manual `inspected_at` strictly after `created_at`, computed hours, compared to 24/12. Record IDs of notes: CTX-... shown in the table.

## Traps and edge cases
- Drone inspections shortly after the note do NOT count (e.g. WELL-0332 INS-0332-0212 drone 2026-12-03 14:45, 2.6 h after the note; WELL-0052 next is drone 2026-12-31; WELL-0163 INS-0163-0165 drone 11-05; WELL-0362 INS-0362-0211 drone 12-24; WELL-0189 INS-0189-0216 drone). A naive "any inspection" answer wrongly marks WELL-0332 Met.
- Routine scheduled maintenance counts as a response (any maintenance job on the well) - e.g. WELL-0165, WELL-0091, WELL-0189 were Met by scheduled jobs whose `started_at` is simply the next morning 08:00.
- Threshold change on 1 Jan 2027: seven reports (WELL-0324, 0330, 0599, 0516, 0425, 0465, 0137) fall under the 12 h rule; WELL-0599 (16.4 h) and WELL-0516 (15.0 h) are Missed only because of it.
- Responses must start AFTER the note: a job that started before the note does not count (none of the 26 relied on this, but WELL-0362's drone at 07:45 and WELL-0401's earlier drone are before/not eligible).
- WELL-0332 (the Q9.4 well): note 2026-12-03 12:10Z, no eligible response -> Missed. WELL-0332 has NO H2S/ppm reading for 3 Dec 2026: the note text carries no ppm value and its last maintenance job before then is MNT-0332-033 on 20 Nov 2026; the only ppm figures in the dataset are crew-monitor maxima inside older repair reports (WS-03 lines) - relevant to Q9 turn 4.
- Times are UTC; no timezone conversion is needed. No note is exactly on a deadline boundary.
- Reports in other note types (`correction`, `operator_feedback`) do not mention gas.
- Late-entered records (-B/-L): none belong to the gas wells.
