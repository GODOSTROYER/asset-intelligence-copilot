## Rules applied
- operations-sop-rev2.md **OPS-2 Temporary bypass**: allowed only on a well named in an MOC memo that has not expired; a memo that extends an earlier memo replaces its expiry date. A bypass is in place from the operator note that puts it in until the operator note that takes it out. Violation if the well is in no MOC, or the bypass is still in place after the expiry date (end of that day), including one still in place today.
- moc-2026-031 / -036 / -044 for the wells, effective dates and expiry. Note to well mapping via `asset_id`; area names from `fleet/areas.json`.

## Method
Searched every `context.jsonl`, `maintenance.jsonl`, `inspections.jsonl`, `regulatory-events.jsonl` and all maintenance-report .md files for "bypass", "temporary", "check valve". Only operator notes in `context.jsonl` mention bypasses: 21 notes on 12 wells. No bypass wording exists in maintenance jobs or reports, so the question's "maintenance / reports" adds nothing. Paired in/out notes per well in time order. Tests: in-date >= memo effective date (all pass); out-date <= expiry end-of-day, or, if still in, today 2027-01-15 vs expiry.

## Traps and edge cases
- MOC-044 only extends MOC-031. WELL-0249 (MOC-036) looks as if it should be covered by "the extension" but is not; WELL-0087 and WELL-0158 (MOC-036) likewise expired 15 Dec.
- WELL-0013 is Allowed ONLY because 044 replaced the 30 Nov expiry (bypass stayed in until 8 Jan 2027). Ignoring 044 would wrongly flag WELL-0013 and WELL-0248.
- WELL-0118 (in MOC-031) and WELL-0388 (in MOC-036) are named in memos but have NO bypass note: no bypass, no violation, not in the table.
- WELL-0405 and WELL-0561 are in no memo; WELL-0405's later "Walked the flowline ... nothing to report" note is not a removal, so its bypass is still in place.
- Varied wording for in ("Temporary bypass in place", "Bypass installed", "Put the bypass in", "Crew had the bypass installed") and out ("Took the bypass out", "Bypass removed", "Bypass pulled", "WELL-0284: valve work finished and bypass removed" - this last one has no "Took out" phrasing).
- Expiry is end of the day; none are borderline (closest outs: WELL-0284 on 10 Dec and WELL-0461 on 12 Dec versus 15 Dec).
- Every bypass-in date is after the memo effective date (20 Oct for 031, 10 Nov for 036); WELL-0476 in on 22 Oct is the closest.
- A bypass that is already out today but overstayed (0087, 0158) still counts for the "past or present" list, and its status is Expired.
