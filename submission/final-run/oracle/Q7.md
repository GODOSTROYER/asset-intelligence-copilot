- Notes older than 2026-12-15 (e.g. stale "rattling" notes from 2025) are out of the window.
- "Scheduled" jobs (lubrication, valve service, annual flowline test) are not repairs; a corrective job after the note removes the well (12 wells removed).
- Spill clean-ups ("Flowline leak causing spill") must not be offered as the predicted repair; the user excluded spill clean-up. (None of the 12 dropped wells was dropped by a spill job alone.)
- Recalled pump already replaced (WELL-0302/0345/0340/0460/0595) or ESP wells (not covered by the recall).

- Ranking purely by note count (e.g. WELL-0023, 0270, 0182 with 3 notes) misses the planted ESP decline and recall signals.
