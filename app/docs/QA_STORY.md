## 45-second spoken explanation

“We started with failures that could produce confident but wrong answers: decimal threshold boundaries, historical records, missing evidence, and model plans that contradicted the user’s explicit scope. We kept those failures visible, fixed the behavior, and reran independent checks against raw sample records and mocked provider responses. The domain suite grew from 86 cases with 11 failures to 109 passing, while the provider checks passed 27 offline cases and the targeted hosted rerun passed three routing cases. One browser-found fleet-status issue has an offline regression, but its UI retest is still pending. These results show local hardening against the supplied sample and defined fixtures; they do not claim final-dataset or production acceptance.”

## Evidence boundary

The domain oracle reads supplied sample records directly, and controlled fixtures cover boundary and incomplete-data cases. Provider offline checks use mocked transport and fake runtime keys. Hosted U17–U19 is a small targeted rerun, not a full live suite. The final dataset is unreleased; final-data and end-to-end evidence must be recorded separately when available. Keep initial failures, limitations, and pending retests visible when updating this story.
